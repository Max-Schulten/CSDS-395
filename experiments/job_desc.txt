Business Analyst I – Digital Banking & Operations
KeyBank | Cleveland, OH (Hybrid – 3 days onsite)
Full-Time | Entry Level | Posted: April 2026

ABOUT THE ROLE
KeyBank's Retail Banking Technology group is seeking a Business Analyst I to support digital product
initiatives across our consumer banking platform. In this role, you will work closely with product owners,
engineers, and operations teams to translate business needs into clear requirements, track delivery
through Agile sprints, and help surface data-driven insights that improve the customer experience.
This is an ideal opportunity for a recent graduate or early-career professional looking to grow within a
large financial institution with a strong internal development culture.

RESPONSIBILITIES
- Elicit, document, and maintain business and functional requirements through stakeholder interviews,
  workshops, and process observation.
- Author user stories, acceptance criteria, and process flow diagrams (BPMN or equivalent) in
  collaboration with product and engineering teams.
- Analyze data from customer-facing digital channels (web, mobile) to identify trends, anomalies, and
  improvement opportunities using SQL and Excel.
- Build and maintain dashboards and recurring reports in Tableau or Power BI to support business and
  operations leadership.
- Participate in Agile ceremonies including sprint planning, daily standups, retrospectives, and
  stakeholder demos.
- Conduct gap analyses between current-state and future-state processes; present findings and
  recommendations to business stakeholders.
- Coordinate UAT activities, including test case preparation, defect tracking in Jira, and sign-off
  documentation.
- Support ad hoc analytical and reporting requests from the retail banking division.

REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS
- Bachelor's degree in Business Administration, Information Systems, Finance, or a related field.
- 0–2 years of experience in a business analyst, operations, or data-related role (internship experience
  counts).
- Proficiency in Microsoft Excel, including PivotTables and lookup functions.
- Familiarity with SQL for querying and analyzing structured data.
- Exposure to Agile/Scrum methodology and project tracking tools such as Jira or Confluence.
- Strong written and verbal communication skills with the ability to present findings clearly to both
  technical and non-technical audiences.

PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS
- Experience with Tableau, Power BI, or comparable data visualization platforms.
- Familiarity with BPMN notation or process mapping tools (Lucidchart, Visio, etc.).
- Exposure to the financial services or banking industry.
- Working knowledge of Python or other scripting languages for data manipulation.
- IIBA ECBA certification or active pursuit thereof.
- GPA of 3.5 or above from an accredited institution.

COMPENSATION & BENEFITS
- Salary range: $58,000 – $68,000 annually, commensurate with experience.
- Annual performance bonus eligibility.
- Comprehensive health, dental, and vision insurance (eligible from day one).
- 401(k) with company match up to 5%.
- Tuition reimbursement up to $5,250 per year.
- 15 days PTO + 10 paid holidays annually.
- Access to KeyBank's professional development and mentorship programs.
- Employee banking benefits including fee waivers and preferred rates.

WORK ENVIRONMENT
This is a hybrid position based out of KeyBank's headquarters in Cleveland, Ohio. Team members are
expected onsite Tuesday through Thursday, with flexibility to work remotely on Mondays and Fridays.
Occasional travel to branch or partner locations may be required (less than 10%).
